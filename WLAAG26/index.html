Enterprise GIS and the City

Dane Atkins, PhD

GIS Analyst, City of Cedar Rapids

Transportation Geography, Perception of Risk

Abstract

What is an Enterprise GIS, and why should you care? This presentation draws upon two years of work experience as a geospatial analyst supporting local government to help answer these questions.

More formally, I aim to provide an autoethnographic reflection of several real-world projects from ideation to implementation. In particular, this reflection should make clear the distinctions between an Enterprise GIS and common classroom GIS environments.

Ultimately, perhaps even more important than any particular technological skills, my reflections identify the professional skills that appear to be most valued by local government entities: collaboration, workflow design, and continuous learning.
